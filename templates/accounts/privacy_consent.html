Philippine Data Privacy Consent & Clinical Notice

Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) Compliance
Before participating in mental health screenings and utilizing AI-assisted wellness recommendations, please review your legal rights and our clinical safety standards.
1. Collection and Processing of Health Information

GabAI collects subjective questionnaire responses concerning psychological wellness, daily routines, stress markers, and developmental traits. In compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and the National Privacy Commission (NPC) regulations, your sensitive personal information is strictly protected through role-based access control, encrypted channels, and confidential clinical record boundaries.

2. Artificial Intelligence Clinical Boundaries

The Artificial Intelligence component functions exclusively as an analytical aid for licensed physicians and psychiatrists. AI DOES NOT:

  • Diagnose psychiatric illnesses or medical conditions.
  • Prescribe medications or clinical treatment regimes.
  • Release clinical guidance to you without review and authorization by an attending physician.
  • Override immediate crisis or suicide prevention safety protocols.
3. Emergency Crisis Protocols

If you indicate immediate self-harm risk or crisis during an assessment, our system will immediately provide 24/7 human emergency helpline contacts (including the National Center for Mental Health Crisis Hotline 1553) and dispatch an urgent triage notification to our clinical safety team.

4. Rights of the Data Subject

You hold the right to be informed, access your clinical records, rectify erroneous data, and request consultation with an attending medical specialist at any time regardless of screening score.
